Valeria Piazza

Principal Investigator (Titular “B”)

Centro de Investigaciones en Óptica (CIO)

I am interested in cellular and tissue morphology, both from the perspective of microscopy techniques for visualization and from the standpoint of the fundamental mechanisms underlying disease, particularly those affecting the nervous system.

I am especially interested in the organization of the cytoskeleton in the sound-sensing cells of the inner ear and in microtubule-related dysfunctions associated with myelin.
