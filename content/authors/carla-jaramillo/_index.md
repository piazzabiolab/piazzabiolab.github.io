Carla María Jaramillo R. is a biomedical engineer from the Instituto Tecnológico Metropolitano (ITM) in Medellín, Colombia. She later completed a Master’s degree in Applied Sciences and Engineering at the Division of Sciences and Engineering of the University of Guanajuato, where she worked with epithelial and oligodendrocyte cell lines, applying pharmacological treatments to reproduce structural defects associated with a neurodegenerative disease.
She is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Optical Sciences at the Centro de Investigaciones en Óptica (CIO), where her work focuses on two main projects. The first centers on the analysis of biomolecular aspects of the cytoskeleton, particularly  post-translational modifications of tubulin that may be linked to the defects observed in the neurodegenerative disease studied during her master’s research. The second project involves the development and application of a spectral microscopy technique to elucidate early changes in myelin that often go unnoticed with conventional techniques.
