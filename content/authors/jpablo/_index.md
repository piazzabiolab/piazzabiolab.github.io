Undergraduate Bionanotechnology Engineering student at Universidad IBERO León with a strong interest in the intersection between biology, technology, data, and sustainability. My academic path has led me to explore molecular biology, microbiology, biotechnology, biochemistry, genetics, environmental science, and computational approaches to biological systems. I am particularly interested in understanding how biological knowledge can be transformed into technological solutions for scientific environmental.

My experience combines academic research, laboratory work, and scientific projects. Through my work in microbiology, I have gained experience in the analysis of potable, residual, and treated water, working with laboratory procedures, microbiological analysis, data recording, interpretation of results, scientific reporting, and applicable Mexican Official Standards (NOMs).

My research interests span molecular biology, microbiology, biotechnology, genetics, genomics, bioinformatics, environmental biotechnology, and sustainability. I am especially interested in the relationship between biological systems and their environments, as well as in the potential of biotechnology to address environmental challenges.
