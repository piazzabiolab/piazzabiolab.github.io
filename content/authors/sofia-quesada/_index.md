Undergraduate student in Bionanotechnology Engineering at Universidad Iberoamericana León (2022-2026) with a special interest in the study of cellular mechanisms for their application in the area of ​​oncology and personalized medicine.

I was accepted into the ENLACE Summer Research Program 2025 at UCSD, where I joined the Department of Cellular and Molecular Medicine, specifically in Dr. Ghosh's laboratory. There, I became involved in several ongoing projects and practiced techniques such as gel preparation and western blot, while using equipment including flow cytometry and the citation system. At the end of the program, I delivered a presentation titled "An intrinsic PKA modulatory motif in GIV/Girdin regulates cAMP dynamics during microbial clearance in macrophages," which was the main project I was involved in.

I performed my social service at the Hospital General de Alta Especialidad de León, where I acquired practical experience in clinical analysis laboratories areas including uroanalysis, toxicology, inmunology, microbiology and molecular biology, emphasizing in hematology tests with flow cytometry.
