---
title: PhD student Carla Maria Jaramillo image, has been selected as the WINNER of the Mexican Association of Microscopy and Microanalysis (AMMM
date: 2026-07-09

---
 The photograph titled “Duality of the Cytoskeleton: Stability and Dynamism,” by PhD student Carla Maria Jaramillo, has been selected as the WINNER and will be featured in the 2027 Calendar of the Mexican Association of Microscopy and Microanalysis (AMMM). The work will also be included in the Art and Science Exhibition: “Microscopy Without Borders: Nature and Peace,” which will be on display during the XIV National Microscopy Congress 2026 in Texcoco, State of Mexico.
